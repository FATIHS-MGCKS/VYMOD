# VYMOD Product Vision

**Tagline:** your car. your rules.

Premium consumer vehicle security and intelligence powered by SYNQDRIVE Core.

## User value

- Unified view of vehicle health, activity, and intelligence
- Privacy-respecting controls over location and data sharing
- Multilingual experience (German-first market, global-ready architecture)
- Future security capabilities gated by explicit safety and legal review

## Non-goals (P0 / near term)

- Direct vehicle immobilization or safety-critical commands without approved architecture
- Standalone analytics pipeline duplicating Core
- Blockchain publication of personal or location data

## Success metrics (future)

Engagement, trust (privacy settings usage), incident rates—not defined in P0.

## Dependencies

- SYNQDRIVE entitlement and telemetry APIs
- Legal review for location and driver analytics
- MASVS/ASVS implementation when apps ship
